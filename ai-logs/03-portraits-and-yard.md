# 人物与后院的实际生成记录

工具为 Codex ImageGen。首次实机版中，秦军户和陈捕头共用官差立绘。两人分别是愿意开口的证人、施压的捕头，容易认混。制作阶段改为生成一张六人等格人物画，裁出独立头像，用于订单和剧情。日间铺面与夜里木箱采用同一手绘方向。该项是制作中的视觉修订。

## 六名人物的原始输入

A carefully aligned 3 columns by 2 rows grid of SIX DISTINCT character portraits. Overall image is wide landscape 3:2, each panel a perfectly square full bleed individual illustration. Absolutely no margins, no gaps, no borders, no text or letters, no logos, no panel overlaps. Historical Chinese storybook gouache, muted golden light and hand brushed rich pigments, realistic expressive stylized faces, original game portrait art. All characters are framed from waist/chest up with FULL HEAD visible, slight air above head, left three-quarter portrait composition within their own square, clear silhouettes. TOP LEFT elderly Chinese farmer, gray beard, straw hat, indigo cotton, sunny wheat fields. TOP MIDDLE lively young Chinese woman cook Xiao He with tidy bun, light olive linen crossed robe, rolled sleeves and red apron, tavern kitchen. TOP RIGHT Chinese mountain hunter, short beard, fur vest forest green with bow slung on his back, distant pines. BOTTOM LEFT young weathered military quartermaster Qin, olive quilted soldier jacket and helmet, worried honest eyes and square jaw, muted army camp backdrop. BOTTOM MIDDLE chief constable Chen, middle aged corrupt county officer, slight calculating smile, thin neat mustache, oxblood Ming-style official coat and black wingless folded official cap, deep red dark timber door behind him. BOTTOM RIGHT mysterious middle aged outsider merchant traveler, narrow face, charcoal woven traveling hat and blue-gray cape, misty dusk street. Painterly textile detail, cinematic chiaroscuro; elegant Ming era fold construction but fantasy historical setting. Strict grid uniform equal six squares, head well-centered and natural hands if shown.

## 黄昏后院的原始输入

Wide 16:9 storybook gouache background plate for historical Chinese mystery game, matching hand-painted golden-light blacksmith artwork with rich natural textures. Inside a small backyard behind an old Ming-style timber workshop at dusk in a riverside Chinese county. A square weather-worn wooden chest with simple rusty clasp sits prominent on the LEFT by a workbench and stacked iron scraps, discarded bundle of cloth. Above it a small amber paper lantern tucked under dark roof beams casts a pool of light. Beyond the open wooden gate one can see blue slate tiles and dusk fog. RIGHT half in unobtrusive indigo shadow to accommodate interface later. The courtyard feels lived-in and quiet, not melodramatic horror. No people, no text, no symbols, no logo. Cinematic diagonal of cool evening light vs warm lantern, painterly historical narrative game environment.
